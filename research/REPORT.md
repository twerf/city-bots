# City bots research report

Generated by `research/lab.mjs` and `research/check.mjs` on cached Yahoo bars:
- **1-minute data:** 20 sessions, 2026-10-29 to 2026-11-25. This is the timeframe the live bots trade on.
- **5-minute data:** 40 older sessions, 2026-09-02 to 2026-10-28. None of them overlap the 1-minute month.

Every trade pays **0.02% slippage on each side plus 0.01% fees on each side** (0.06% round trip). The tests use the same `stepBars` engine the live bots use.

## Method
- **Walk-forward (`lab.mjs`):** the first 5 days only build track records. After that the lab tunes on all past days, tests on the next 3 unseen days, and slides forward. The tuning is a greedy search: starting from the current bots, add whichever idea most improves the funded-portfolio return on the training days, and repeat.
- **Cross-check (`check.mjs`):** an idea is kept if, on its own, it beat the current bots on both the funded-portfolio and the even-split measure. The ideas are picked on one dataset and the bundle is tested on the other, which it never saw.
- **Baselines:** the current bots with costs, an even split across every bot, and buy-and-hold SPY.

## Results

### 1. Greedy walk-forward tuning does NOT work
On 12 unseen 1-minute days it made **−2.24%**, against **+1.12%** for the current bots. With 3-12 training days, the search mostly finds noise. Don't auto-tune on short history.

### 2. Cross-check
- **Picked on the 1-minute month, tested on the 35 unseen 5-minute days:** +0.43% (worst drop −1.37%) against −0.47% for the current bots (worst drop −3.39%). The even split went from −1.70% to −0.59%, and SPY buy-and-hold made +1.89%. **This is the bundle that ships.**
- **Picked on the 5-minute days, tested on the unseen 1-minute days:** −1.33% against +1.12% for the current bots. The worst drop was much smaller (−1.59% vs −4.16%), and the even split improved (−0.26% vs −1.71%).

### 3. What held up consistently
These ideas helped on their own in both datasets, on both measures:
- **Trading only in the morning window (9:45-11:15):** this was the strongest one.
- **Volume and 5-minute-trend confirmation on entries.**
- **Dropping the trend bots.**
- **The yesterday-high/low strategy:** a small gain.
- **A max of 3 trades per bot per day:** a small gain.

### 4. What didn't hold up
- **Market-mood filter:** mixed results.
- **ATR-sized stops:** helped the even split but hurt the funded portfolio on the 1-minute data.
- **Trailing stops:** worse on both datasets.
- **30-minute cool-off after a loss:** about flat.
- **Equal weighting for funded bots:** worse than rank weighting.
- **Gap and pullback strategies:** each failed on one of the two datasets.

### 5. The honest bottom line
After costs, no strategy has a reliable edge:
- **Trend bots:** profit factor 0.60 at ~87 trades a day. Costs eat them alive.
- **Opening-range breakout:** profit factor 1.10 on the 1-minute month, 0.81 on the older days.
- **Gap fill:** profit factor 1.57 on the 1-minute month, 0.66 on the older days.

The shipped settings trade about 3x less and roughly halve the worst drop. They've done better than the current bots on unseen days, but the gain is small and the sample is short. Live paper trading is the real out-of-sample test.

## Shipped settings (`SHIPPED` in city.mjs)
- **Strategies:** og, fade, pdhl, gap, vwapb. Trend was dropped.
- **Entries:** 9:45-11:15 only, with volume and 5-minute-trend confirmation, max 3 per bot per day.
- **Funding:** the top 8, ranked on a steadiness score (return ÷ volatility). Any bot with 3 red days in a row is benched.

Re-run everything: `node research/data.mjs --refresh && node research/lab.mjs && node research/check.mjs`

## Round 2: parameter sweeps + ship check (`sweep.mjs`, `replay.mjs`)
Each setting was swept one at a time around the shipped values. Picks used a neighbour-smoothed score on the 1-minute month, then were checked on the older 5-minute days.

### Sweep results
- **Flat plateaus.** The shipped values sit on these, so there's nothing to tune:
  - profit lock (1-5%)
  - shrink
  - bench length
  - max trades 2-6
- **Morning-window end:** 630-780 all land between about +1.7% and +3.5% on the 1-minute month and 0 to +0.7% on the older days. The 11:15 end is mid-plateau.
- **Gap minimum:** 0.2-0.3% is fine; 0.4%+ is worse on the older days. Kept 0.3%.
- **Fade RSI:** 20/25/30 are all similar. Kept 25.
- **Funded bot count:** fewer funded bots means bigger swings both ways. Top 4 makes more but has a bigger worst drop (−2.2% vs −1.4%); it's a risk choice, not an edge. Kept 8.
- **Ranking window:** 15 days was best on the 1-minute month but ~0% on the older days. Kept 10.
- **Cluster caps (max 2 per ticker, 2-3 per group):** helped one dataset and hurt the other. Not shipped.
- **Combining every individual sweep winner:** +8.1% on the 1-minute month, but **−0.6%** on the older days, vs +0.4% for the shipped settings. That's overfitting in action. **No sweep changes shipped.**

### Month replay ($20, rank-funded top 8, costs included)
- **1-minute month** (ideas were picked here): old bots $20.50, new bots $20.52. Even split $19.68, SPY $18.73.
- **Older 5-minute days** (never tuned on): old bots $19.91, new bots $20.09. Even split $19.66, **SPY $20.38**.

### Ship rule
The new bots beat the old bots and the even split on unseen days, with a smaller worst drop. They did **not** beat buy-and-hold SPY on the unseen days, because the market rose in that stretch. v3 stays live as the better of the two bot versions, but under the strict rule it doesn't pass.

## Round 3 (`round3.mjs`): realistic fills, luck check, scanner, risk sizing, options view
Datasets: the 1-minute month (15 test days after a 5-day warm-up) and 35 older 5-minute days. None of the round-3 changes were tuned on either one. The universe grew to 64 tickers, and the 1-minute bars now come from `archive/` merged with Yahoo.

| | 1-min month | older 5-min days |
|---|---|---|
| current, fills at signal close | $20.52 (luck check 79%) | $20.08 (62%) |
| **current, next-bar fills (shipped)** | **$20.47 (77%)** | **$20.07 (61%)** |
| + risk-sized bets | $20.17 (67%) | $20.04 (57%) |
| + scanner (top 15 of 64) | $20.21 (72%) | $19.19 (0%) |
| control: all 64 tickers, no scanner | $20.52 (78%) | $20.01 (51%) |
| + scanner + risk-sized | $19.98 (48%) | $19.29 (0%) |
| hold SPY | $18.73 | $20.38 |
| options view (estimate) | $17.90 (worst drop −51%) | $11.62 (worst drop −73%) |

### What each change did
- **Realistic fills** (next bar's open; stop first when a bar hits both): about −0.05 to −0.2 percentage points over a month. Shipped as a realism fix, not as an improvement.
- **Luck check** (5,000 bootstrap resamples): **every result is under 80%**, so none of it can be told apart from luck yet. These strategies have at least 30 trades: og, fade, gap, and pdhl on the 1-minute month. vwapb (28 / 0 trades) and pdhl on the older days (26) are too thin to trust.
- **Scanner:** worse on both datasets, and very bad on the older days. In-play names rotate daily, so the bots that get funded have almost no track record, and the gap bots on big-gap days lost. The all-64 control shows that simply adding tickers doesn't help either. **Not shipped.**
- **Risk-sized bets:** smaller worst drop but lower return on both datasets. **Not shipped.**
- **Options view:** the same bets as 1-day-to-expiry options (Black-Scholes estimate, vol = yesterday's realized × 1.1, 2% bid/ask each side) **lose money on both datasets**, with drops of 50-70%. A small stock edge doesn't survive options' time decay plus spreads. Shipped as a display-only number on the City tab.
- **Archive:** `archive.yml` saves every ticker's 1-minute bars each evening. It was seeded with the last 20 sessions (~340 KB/day).

**Ship check:** no strategy change passed. Only the realism fix (next-bar fills) and the display-only options view were shipped.

## Weekly report card (`weekly.mjs`, `.github/workflows/weekly.yml`)
Runs on Fridays at 22:15 UTC. Each run:
- compares the live paper days (`state.days`, new in state v5) with the replay engine's prediction for the same days, using the archive + Yahoo data
- re-runs the research on all data so far: bots vs even split vs SPY, luck check, and each idea applied alone to the shipped settings
- posts to Discord #city-bots through the `DISCORD_WEBHOOK` repo secret

Ideas are only *suggested* when they beat the current bots on both datasets and pass a luck check of at least 80%. Nothing is ever shipped automatically.

## Round 4: the "slow lane" (`slow.mjs`, daily bars 1993-2026)
These are daily strategies, each one a separate bot, using dividend-adjusted Yahoo daily bars. Fills happen at the next day's open (the overnight bot buys at the close and sells at the next open), with the same costs as the day-trading bots.

The test is a walk-forward: settings are tuned only on the years before each test year, and the test years are 2005-2026 (5,510 unseen days). The luck check is a 20-day-block bootstrap.

| bot | per year | worst drop | trades/yr | win | PF | beats-SPY luck | 2008 | 2020 | 2022 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hold SPY | +10.5% | −55.2% | 0 | – | – | – | −36.8% | +18.3% | −18.2% |
| QQQ trend (200-day avg) | +9.5% | −27.0% | 4 | 36% | 3.71 | 36% | −16.5% | +31.1% | −12.8% |
| Momentum rotation (top 3 ETFs) | +8.9% | −27.7% | 12 | 54% | 2.30 | 30% | −10.9% | +20.5% | −8.7% |
| Mix of best low-overlap bots | +8.2% | **−18.6%** | – | – | – | 21% | **+5.6%** | +7.3% | −4.2% |
| SPY trend | +6.7% | −25.5% | 3 | 32% | 3.42 | 8% | −0.9% | −0.9% | −14.3% |
| Mix + volatility targeting | +6.6% | −14.1% | – | – | – | 9% | +2.6% | +8.4% | −4.5% |
| Even split of all slow bots | +5.5% | −21.2% | – | – | – | 1% | −9.6% | +9.4% | −9.8% |
| QQQ dip buying | +4.8% | −12.5% | 11 | 64% | 2.02 | 9% | +28.5% | +1.1% | +10.7% |
| SPY dip buying | +2.8% | −27.1% | 17 | 63% | 1.39 | 1% | 0.0% | −6.1% | +0.7% |
| Overnight | −6.8% | −80.8% | 250 | 50% | 0.89 | 0% | −26.0% | −1.6% | −25.5% |

**Ship check: NOTHING PASSES.** No slow bot beat simply holding SPY on return with at least 80% luck-check confidence, so the slow lane was not added to the live City.

**What it does show:**
- Every trend/rotation bot and both mixes are profitable beyond luck (luck check of 98-100% that the return is above zero).
- **The low-overlap mix earned about 80% of SPY's return with about a third of the worst crash.** It was +5.6% in 2008 while SPY fell 37%. Its Sharpe was 0.78 vs SPY's 0.62.
- Even after round-trip costs, the overnight bot loses badly: 250 trades a year × 0.06% each eats about 15% a year.

If the rule is relaxed to "similar return, much smaller crashes" (for example, higher Sharpe and less than half SPY's worst drop on unseen years), the mix would pass.
